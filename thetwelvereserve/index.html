Collector questions · The XII Reserve

Buying or selling an exceptional car?

Can The XII Reserve help me find a Ferrari or Porsche that is not advertised?

Yes. Our private sourcing service starts with a precise brief, including model, preferred colors, mileage, specification, budget and timing. We then evaluate potential cars through dealer and collector relationships. Availability is confirmed individually.

Can I offer my Ferrari or Lamborghini without publicly advertising it?

Yes. Start with a confidential vehicle submission. We review condition, mileage, specification and records before discussing a potential direct purchase, private brokerage or consignment approach.
