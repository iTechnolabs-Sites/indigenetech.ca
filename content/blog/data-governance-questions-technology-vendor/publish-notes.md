### Unresolved
- The Rank Math FAQ block has not yet been confirmed to render on indigenetech.ca. Preview a draft once before relying on it.
- The post commits IndigeneTech to answering these questions in writing. The user should confirm the team is willing to do that, and ideally publish IndigeneTech's own answers.
- Which privacy laws apply to First Nation governments, corporations and service organizations is not settled in one rule; the post deliberately defers to legal advice.
- No FNIGC review: if IndigeneTech wants to reference OCAP® prominently, FNIGC's training is a sensible step for the team.
